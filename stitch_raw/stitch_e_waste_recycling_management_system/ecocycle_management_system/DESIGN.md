## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for an institutional, forward-thinking environmental management platform built around e-waste auditing, logistics, and scheduled recycling. The aesthetic fuses **Clean Corporate Modernism** with **Eco-Minimalism**, avoiding faux-natural clichés (leaf graphics, heavy earth-brown textures) in favor of clinical precision, sustainable vitality, and high-legibility utility.

### Brand Personality & Audience
- **Target Audience:** College campus community members (students, faculty, administrators) booking pick-ups, alongside logistics personnel and campus sustainability managers overseeing recycling workflows.
- **Tone & Mood:** Methodical, transparent, ecological, responsible, and systematically organized.
- **Visual Stance:** Crisp white surfaces, structural slate boundaries, energetic emerald callouts, and crystalline mint highlights. The interface communicates rapid operational throughput and environmental accountability without sacrificing administrative rigor.

## Colors

The system relies on a high-contrast, nature-grounded spectrum engineered for complex data tables, public scheduling workflows, and status tracking.

### Primary Spectrum (Emerald & Forest)
- **Primary Base (`#16a34a`):** Primary interactions, successful states, and main CTAs.
- **Primary Deep (`#15803d`):** Hover states, interactive depth, and high-emphasis icons.
- **Forest Base (`#052e16`):** Deep brand grounding, executive admin navbars, high-contrast headings, and high-impact hero sections.

### Mint Accents & Tint Surfaces
- **Mint Accent (`#86efac`):** Visual badges, chart accents, progress bar highlights, focus indicators.
- **Mint Tint (`#dcfce7`):** Background wash for active states, positive badges, and callout notification containers.

### Neutrals & Structural Grays
- **Canvas Base (`#ffffff`):** Pure white for cards, modals, and input fields.
- **Canvas Muted (`#f8fafc`):** Application shell background, table zebra striping, and inactive segments.
- **Border Default (`#e2e8f0`):** Hairline boundary for structural isolation.
- **Border Hover (`#cbd5e1`):** Subtle feedback border for interactive surfaces.
- **Text Slate (`#0f172a`):** High-readability foreground for primary body and titles.
- **Text Muted (`#64748b`):** Tertiary information, metadata tags, field placeholders, and helper text.

### Functional Status Indicators
- **Pending / Action Required:** Amber `#d97706` (Text/Icon), `#fef3c7` (Surface).
- **Approved / Scheduled:** Cobalt Blue `#2563eb` (Text/Icon), `#dbeafe` (Surface).
- **Collected / Recycled:** Emerald `#16a34a` (Text/Icon), `#dcfce7` (Surface).
- **Cancelled / Rejected:** Rose `#dc2626` (Text/Icon), `#fee2e2` (Surface).

## Typography

The typography pairings unify **Plus Jakarta Sans** for prominent, contemporary structural headlines and **Inter** for data grids, forms, and administrative logs.

- **Headlines & Titles:** Set with tighter negative letter-spacing to deliver confident, high-density titles across responsive viewports.
- **Body & Data Displays:** Use neutral metrics from Inter to preserve tabular alignment, monospaced-like readability of item weights (kg, lbs), tracking serial numbers, and pick-up timestamps.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Labels leverage uppercase transform or heightened medium-to-semibold weights to ensure status indicators and filter tokens remain distinguishable even at compact sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The system runs on a **Fluid-Responsive 12-Column Grid** system anchored by an 8pt architectural rhythm.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Geometry
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4 fluid columns, `1rem` outer canvas margin, `1rem` inter-column gutter. Single-column card stacking.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 8 fluid columns, `1.5rem` canvas margin, `1.5rem` gutter. Admin tables scroll horizontally with frozen action columns; category cards reflow into 2-up grids.
- **Desktop (>= 1025px):** 12 fluid columns constrained to a max-width container of `1280px` (`80rem`), `2rem` outer margin, `1.5rem` gutter. Fixed vertical navigation alongside dynamic operational dashboards.

### Spacing Hierarchy
- Use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) strictly for internal pill badges, compact inputs, and icon-to-label gaps.
- Use `space-md` (16px) for interior card padding, stack layout margins, and standard form element vertical separation.
- Use `space-lg` (24px) for hero card paddings, modal interiors, and dashboard widget layouts.
- Use `space-xl` (40px) for boundary offsets between discrete sections (e.g., separating summary stat grids from administrative data logs).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined **Borders-Over-Shadows** philosophy, supplemented by crisp, low-spread ambient shadows. This prevents the interface from feeling muddy while anchoring high-density logistics tables.

### Depth Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** `#f8fafc` canvas background without elevation or border.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Surface):** Pure `#ffffff` surface, bounded by a 1px solid `#e2e8f0` outline, supported by an ultra-subtle tinted shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Interactive Containers):** Raised cards on interaction. Outlines shift to `#cbd5e1` with a soft elevation lift: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Flyouts, Popovers, & Drawers):** Bounded by `#e2e8f0` border, resting on `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 4 (Modal Dialogs & Overlays):** Deep modal elevation `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Backdrops use `rgba(5, 46, 22, 0.4)` (deep forest green translucent wash) coupled with a `4px` backdrop blur to reinforce the eco-brand context during modal focus.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry balance. Elements feel modern, friendly, and approachable while maintaining the structural sharpness demanded by professional data applications.

### Corner Radius Allocation
- **Base Components (`rounded`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Input fields, single select menus, action buttons, table row selection highlights, and navigation links.
- **Intermediate Cards (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Stat cards, recycling category tiles, request history containers, and nested module panels.
- **Macro Containers (`rounded-xl`, 1.5rem / 24px):** Hero banners, comprehensive logistics modals, and floating notification alerts.
- **Full Pill (`rounded-full`, 9999px):** Status badges, count tags, and operational quick-filter chips.

## Components

Modular React functional component guidelines for implementation:

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Background `#16a34a`, hover `#15803d`, text `#ffffff`, height 40px, padding horizontal 16px, radius 8px, font Inter Semibold 14px. Focus state: `2px solid #86efac` with 2px offset.
- **Secondary Button:** Background `#ffffff`, border 1px solid `#e2e8f0`, text `#0f172a`, hover background `#f8fafc` and border `#cbd5e1`.
- **Danger Button:** Background `#dc2626`, hover `#b91c1c`, text `#ffffff`.

### 2. StatusBadge
- Inline-flex element, padding 2px 10px, radius 9999px (full pill), font Inter Semibold 11px uppercase tracking.
- **Pending:** `#fef3c7` bg, `#d97706` text, with a 6px solid `#d97706` indicator dot.
- **Approved/Scheduled:** `#dbeafe` bg, `#2563eb` text, with a 6px solid `#2563eb` indicator dot.
- **Collected/Recycled:** `#dcfce7` bg, `#15803d` text, with a 6px solid `#16a34a` indicator dot.
- **Cancelled:** `#fee2e2` bg, `#dc2626` text, with a 6px solid `#dc2626` indicator dot.

### 3. StatCard
- Background `#ffffff`, border 1px solid `#e2e8f0`, radius 16px, padding 20px.
- Houses a 44x44px rounded-lg container for eco/e-waste iconography (e.g., mint background `#dcfce7` with emerald icon `#16a34a`).
- Metric value displayed using Plus Jakarta Sans Bold 32px (`#0f172a`), subtitle in Inter Regular 13px (`#64748b`).

### 4. CategoryCard
- Selectable cards for e-waste categories (e.g., Batteries, Computing, Large Appliances, Screens).
- Interactive border: 1.5px solid `#e2e8f0`, shifting to 2px solid `#16a34a` and background `#dcfce7`/20 on active selection. Radius 16px, padding 16px.

### 5. RequestCard
- Summary container for student/faculty pick-up requests. Contains: Request ID, Device Count, Collection Date, Location, and StatusBadge aligned top-right.

### 6. AdminTable & AdminSidebar
- **Sidebar:** Fixed width 260px, surface `#ffffff`, border-right 1px solid `#e2e8f0`. Active navigation item: `#dcfce7` background with `#15803d` label and vertical 3px indicator pill on the active edge.
- **AdminTable:** White surface, border 1px solid `#e2e8f0`, radius 12px, overflow hidden. Table head `#f8fafc`, uppercase text `#64748b` at 12px. Table rows 56px height, border-bottom 1px solid `#e2e8f0`, hover state `#f8fafc`.

### 7. Form Controls & Inputs
- Text inputs, textareas, and selects feature 40px height, 1px solid `#e2e8f0` border, 8px radius, white background.
- Focus ring: 2px solid `#16a34a` with no fuzzy glow, accompanied by a clean transition to crisp slate text.
- Checkboxes: 18x18px, 4px corner radius, checked background `#16a34a` with white checkmark glyph.

### 8. Modal Dialog
- Overlay: `#052e16` at 45% opacity with 4px backdrop-filter blur.
- Container: Max-width 560px, pure white surface, 16px radius, padding 24px, 1px solid `#e2e8f0`, elevation level 4. Header contains Plus Jakarta Sans Headline-SM with dedicated close icon button.

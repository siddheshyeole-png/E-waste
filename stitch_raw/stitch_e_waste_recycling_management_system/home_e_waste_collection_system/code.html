Common E-Waste We Accept

smartphone
Accepted

Mobile Phones & Tablets

Smartphones, iPads, chargers, smartwatches, power leads, and audio accessories.

Key Recoverable Metals:

Gold circuitry, cobalt cells, copper antenna traces, indium tin oxide glass.

Audit Code: EW-01-MOB Free Pick
laptop_mac
Accepted

Computers & Laptops

Desktops, MacBooks, PC towers, keyboards, servers, RAM modules, and GPUs.

Security Handling:

DoD-compliant magnetic degaussing & physical hard drive puncturing included.

Audit Code: EW-02-CMP Free Pick
tv
Accepted

TVs & Monitors

LED, LCD flat displays, old CRT monitors, television units, and display cords.

Environmental Note:

Phosphor & leaded glass isolated in negative pressure cleanrooms.

Audit Code: EW-03-DSP Free Pick
battery_charging_full
Accepted

Batteries & Power Banks

Li-ion packs, dry cells, UPS lead-acid batteries, nickel-cadmium modular cells.

Containment Protocol:

Fire-retardant vermiculite transport boxes used on all dispatched runs.

Audit Code: EW-04-BAT High Priority
print
Accepted

Printers & Scanners

Inkjets, laserjet printers, toner cartridges, photocopiers, and all feed rollers.

Polymer Processing:

Heavy engineered ABS plastics separated and granulated for injection molding.

Audit Code: EW-05-PRN Free Pick
home_iot_device
Accepted

Household Electronics

Microwaves, power cables, Wi-Fi routers, lab scales, remotes, and audio stereos.

Fractional Breakdown:

Transformers stripped for pure oxygen-free high thermal conductivity copper.

Audit Code: EW-06-OTH Free Pick

Why Recycle E-Waste?

Electronics are filled with high-value natural elements, but when discarded in dumpsters, they become persistent biological toxins.
